How Availability & Coverage are Calculated
Observed Duration Total time a host was actively monitored by Prometheus in the selected window.
Availability (Uptime / Observed Duration) × 100%. Unmonitored time is excluded so newly added hosts are not penalized.
Coverage (Observed Duration / Selected Window) × 100%. Hosts with <50% coverage display a Limited data indicator.
Fleet Availability (Total Uptime across Fleet / Total Observed Duration) × 100%.
